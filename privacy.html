Privacy Policy

FF TW Marketing Analytics — internal tool for Garena Free Fire Taiwan marketing team. Last updated: 2026-06-28.

Scope

This document covers FF TW Marketing Analytics, an internal tool used by the Garena Free Fire Taiwan marketing team to read weekly performance data from the team's own official social media accounts (TikTok @freefiretw, and others). The tool is not distributed to the public and does not collect data from third-party users.

Data Accessed

  • Aggregate account statistics of our own official accounts: follower count, total video views, total likes, total video count.
  • Publicly available metadata of our own posts: post ID, title, creation time, view count, like count, comment count, share count.
  • No personal data of end users, viewers, commenters, or third parties is read, stored, or transmitted by this tool.

Data Storage

Numeric metrics are stored locally on the operating team's machines and in internal Garena document storage for the sole purpose of producing internal weekly marketing reports. No data is shared externally or transferred outside the internal team.

Authorization

API access is performed via TikTok's official OAuth flow, authorized by the account owner (Garena Free Fire Taiwan marketing team). Access can be revoked at any time by the account owner via TikTok account settings.

Contact

For questions about this tool's privacy practices, contact the Garena Free Fire Taiwan marketing team.
